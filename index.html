About Us

Where 2000+ rated players come to get dangerous

NextGen TT is built exclusively for competitive players rated 2000 and above — no beginners, no filler drills, just relentless, high-intensity training against opponents who hit as hard as you do. Every rally is a battle. Every session is data-driven, filmed, and dissected together, because watching yourself compete at full speed is the fastest way to find your edge and break your next rating ceiling.
