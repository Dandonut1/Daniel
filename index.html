About Us

A training group built around progress and community

We're a group of coaches and players who believe table tennis is one of the best sports for reflexes, focus, and fun. Whether you're picking up a paddle for the first time or grinding for your next tournament, we build a training plan around your goals — and we film it, because watching yourself play is one of the fastest ways to improve.

🏓

Certified Coaches

National-level coaching staff with decades of combined tournament and teaching experience.

Programs

Training for every stage of the game

Pick a track, or mix and match as you grow. All programs include access to open-play hours and our video library.

🌱
Beginner

Foundations Program

Grip, stance, footwork, and your first rally. Perfect for total beginners of any age.

  • Serve & return basics
  • Ball control drills
  • Fun mini-games to build reflexes
⚡
Intermediate

Performance Program

Sharpen spin, footwork patterns, and shot selection with structured drilling.

  • Topspin & backspin systems
  • Multi-ball training
  • Live match play & scoring tactics
🏆
Advanced / Competitive

Elite Squad

Tournament-focused training for players competing at league or national level.

  • Video-analyzed match play
  • Physical & mental conditioning
  • Tournament travel & league entries
🧒
Ages 6–14

Junior Development

A fun, energetic introduction to the sport built for young players and school groups.

  • Coordination & footwork games
  • Parent progress updates
  • End-of-term fun tournament
☕
Adults

Social & Fitness

Low-pressure, high-energy sessions focused on fitness, fun, and friendly competition.

  • Round-robin match nights
  • Cardio-focused drilling
  • Casual league standings
